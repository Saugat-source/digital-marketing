    A simple social media
    strategy

    1

    Know your audience

    Who are they? What do they like? Which apps do they use?

    2

    Set a clear goal

    More followers, more website visits, or more sales?

    3

    Pick the right platforms

    Be great on two platforms instead of average on six.

    4

    Post useful, regular content

    Plan a content calendar and stay consistent.

    5

    Measure and improve

    Check reach, engagement and clicks — then do more of what works.
